Why Market Bell

Lunch breaks, handled right

Hong Kong, mainland China, Japan and Korea all pause midday. Market Bell knows exactly when trading stops and when it resumes.

Daylight saving, done for you

US and European market hours shift by an hour through the year. No more mental math about what time the open actually lands in your day.

Holidays and half days, built in

Every supported exchange's holiday calendar ships inside the app and is kept current — including shortened trading days.

Never open the app

Home Screen widgets, open alerts and Live Activities put the countdown where you already look. The widget is the product.

Support

Questions, bugs, or a market you want added?

Open Market Bell → Settings → Feedback and send a message directly from the app. It goes straight to the developer's inbox, and it's the fastest way to get a reply.

Common questions — wrong market hours, a widget that isn't refreshing, requesting a market or language — are answered on the Support page. Data handling is covered in the Privacy Policy.
