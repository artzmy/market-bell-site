Support

Market Bell: global stock market open/close countdown for iOS.

Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion?

Open Market Bell → Settings → Feedback, and send a message directly from the app. That's the fastest way to reach the developer; it goes straight to my inbox.

Common questions

  • A market's hours look wrong. Exchange calendars (holidays, half-days) are updated periodically. Let me know the market and date via in-app Feedback and I'll check it.
  • Widget isn't updating. iOS limits how often widgets can refresh in the background. That's a system constraint, not a bug. Reopening the app refreshes it immediately.
  • I want to request a market or language. Send it through in-app Feedback — that's exactly what it's for.
